Aria - app packages
===================

Aria.apk
  Android app. Copy it to a phone and tap it, or send it over WhatsApp/Drive.
  Android asks once to allow "install unknown apps" for whatever you opened
  it from - that is normal for any app that is not from the Play Store.

Aria-Windows.zip
  Windows app. Unzip it anywhere, keep the files together, then double-click
  Aria.exe. It opens Aria in her own clean window, no address bar, nothing to
  install. It is 40 KB because it uses the Edge that Windows 10/11 already
  has, instead of shipping its own browser engine inside.

There is no .ipa in this zip, and it is not possible to make one here.
Apple only lets an iPhone install an app that is signed with an Apple
Development certificate, and building one needs a Mac running Xcode plus a
99 USD/year Apple Developer account. Every "free online ipa maker" either
needs that same Mac, or produces a file a real iPhone refuses to install.

What works on iPhone instead, in about ten seconds:
  open https://alleluia-hash.github.io/aria-chat/ in Safari
  -> Share button -> "Add to Home Screen"
That gives Aria her own icon on the home screen and opens her fullscreen,
with no App Store, no account and no expiry.

Aria needs internet: she looks things up live (weather, prices, web search)
and talks to a free model service. No account, no API key, no tracking.
